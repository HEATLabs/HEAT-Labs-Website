Strengths & Weaknesses

Strengths and weaknesses based on community feedback

Strengths

Excellent Burst Acceleration

The T-62AV has exceptional burst speed, enabling it to quickly contest objectives, relocate between firing positions, and seize advantageous terrain. Mobility is one of the tanks greatest assets in its survival.

High Mobility

Coupled with its high burst speed the T-62 has high mobility. Agile at almost any speed and with a limited ability to drift, skilled commanders will be able to weave in and out of engagements at little risk.

Burst Damage Potential

The T-62AV has an autoreloader which makes it great for dishing out damage in a short space of time. coupled with the potential for ammo upgrades to HEAT rounds, contributions from its rockets from its rockets and a decent ramming modifier, this vehicle has a high DPM output.

Disruption Ability

Players of the T-62AV have several tools at their disposal to make the lives of their enemies miserable. From being able to apply slow and covering large amounts of space in fire this tank excels at distruption, crowd control and AOE damage, especially against heavy and slow tanks.

Respectable survivability

The T-62AV is no slouch when it comes to trading blows. Whilst its HP pool of 2660 may seem standard, It's low profile, sloped armour and general maneuverability means T-62AV drivers will get used to seeing shots bounce as the enemy hits unpredicted angles, especially those who drive erratically.

Weaknesses

Large Weakspots

Players of the T-62AV must remain mindful of their weaknesses. From almost any angle the T-62AV has a critical module that can be hit, skilled commanders may exploit this. A T-62 under accurate fire will not live for long. Avoid becoming the focus of the enemy.

Vulnerable to Crowd Control

Alot of your survivability comes from your ability to keep moving. As a result the T-62 may find matchups against certain vehicles challenging. You should always be on the lookout for enemy mines, grease or other CC equipped tanks. HE FV's and XM1V's can both be challenging enemies to counter.

Sustained Damage

The T-62AV is very much a punch in and out of the fight vehicle. Prolonged engagements will see your damage drop off vastly thanks to the quickly depleted autoreloader. T-62 commanders should keep an eye on their resources and know when they need to back out. You are a dive, not a sustain.

Gun Accuracy

The T-62AV can suffer from on the move accuracy, although some modules can be taken to offset this to a degree this is a fact that T-62AV commanders will have to learn to live with. As a result, at best, the T-62AV is limited to intermediate ranges at most.

Tank Summary

The T-62AV is an exceptionally mobile vehicle built around speed, acceleration, and aggressive repositioning. Its powerful burst mobility allows it to rapidly seize key terrain, relocate between engagements, and isolate vulnerable targets. While its armour and survivability are respectable, the vehicle's extensive module coverage makes reckless pushes into multiple opponents a dangerous proposition.

The T-62AV can be a difficult vehicle to deal with when allowed to use its mobility freely. Commanders will find themselves capable of performing a wide variety of roles, from crowd control and battlefield disruption to acting as an opportunistic scavenger. Opponents will quickly discover that this is not a vehicle you want to face alone, as its opening burst damage and ability to dictate engagements can be difficult to counter.

Commanders of the T-62AV must manage their resources carefully when committing to a fight and remain mindful that much of the vehicle's survivability comes from its ability to keep moving. Mines, grease, tracking shots, and vehicles with strong immobilisation capabilities such as the FV4030 can quickly turn the tide against an overextended T-62AV. Knowing when to disengage from an unfavourable fight is often just as important as knowing when to commit.
